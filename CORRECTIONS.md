# Corrections, withdrawals, and version history

This repository keeps corrections as part of the public record. Archived
versions remain immutable.

## Policy

- Report a suspected error through the repository issue tracker. Give the
  affected claim, the version or commit, and enough detail to reproduce the concern.
- Typographical fixes that do not change a claim may be made on the living
  branch and listed in the next release notes.
- A material correction creates a new version. Its record names the affected
  version and claim, the reason for the change, and the replacement evidence.
- A withdrawn claim stays findable, with a dated notice, the reason, the affected
  versions, and any replacement.
- No archived artifact is silently replaced.
